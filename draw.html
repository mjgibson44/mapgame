Border Draw

Pick a continent. You'll see its coastline with no borders, and one country's name at a time — draw that country where you think it is.

  • Only land counts — the sea is ignored, so it's the borders that matter
  • Up to 100 points per country, for how closely your shape matches
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom in on the small ones
