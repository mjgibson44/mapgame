Border Draw

Pick a continent and you get its coastline — no borders, no names. Draw in every border between its countries, all in one go, then score your map.

  • Nothing tells you which countries you've missed — that's up to you
  • Each country scores up to 100 points: for drawing all of its borders, close to where they really are
  • Stray lines through a country cost it points
  • Only the borders count — the coast is already drawn, and lines out at sea are ignored
  • Works with Apple Pencil: the Pencil draws, fingers move and zoom the map
