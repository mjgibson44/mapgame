Border Draw

Pick a continent and you get its coastline — no borders, no names. Say which country you're drawing, draw its outline, then move on to the next. Nothing is checked until you score your map.

  • Nothing tells you which countries are left — that's up to you
  • Each country scores up to 100 points for how closely your outline matches it; ones you leave out score 0
  • Only land counts: loop out to sea around the coast, it's the borders that matter
  • Works with Apple Pencil: the Pencil draws, fingers move and zoom the map
